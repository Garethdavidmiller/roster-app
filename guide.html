The Buttons

🏖️ AL
Your annual leave balance — Taken · Booked · Remaining · Entitlement. Tap "Record leave in Admin" to record leave you've already booked through the official process.
📝 Admin
Staff self-service — record annual leave, log absence, and view or delete saved changes. You'll need to log in with your name and surname as the password.
☰ Menu
Tap ☰ (top-left) to open the navigation menu. Find the Daily Huddle briefing, guides, Settings, and links to other pages here.
💷 Pay
Opens the Pay Calculator — estimate your take-home pay for any 4-week period.
👥 Team
See the whole team's shifts for any week. Tap CEA, CES or Dispatcher to switch grade. Use ← → to move between weeks.

Push notifications
Android: tap ☰ → Settings → Enable notifications.
iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open from Home Screen and go to ☰ → Settings → Enable notifications. (Must be added to Home Screen first — notifications won't work from the browser tab.)

Add Your Work Email

Open ☰ Menu → ⚙ Settings and add your work email in the 📧 Work Email card. It will be used for password reset when that feature is added — worth setting now so you're ready. Only you and your manager can see it.

Works Offline

Your base roster is stored on your device after first load — the calendar always works without signal. Shift changes recorded by admin and the Huddle link need internet to load.

Logging In

  1. Tap the 📝 Admin button on the main screen
  2. Select your name from the dropdown
  3. Enter your surname as the password — lowercase, no spaces or punctuation
  4. Tap Log In — sessions last 30 days
Example: "D. Smith" → password is smith

Recording a Shift Change

  1. Select the staff member from the top dropdown
  2. Navigate to the week containing the day
  3. Tap a shift type on the day — it turns amber to show it's selected
  4. Enter Start and End time if the type requires one
  5. Add an optional note for context
  6. Tap Save Changes

Apply to Multiple Days

Tick the checkboxes beside several days — the Apply to multiple days at once bar appears at the bottom. Pick a type and tap 3. Apply to ticked days. For a holiday spanning several days, use the Record Annual Leave card instead — pick a date range and tap Record.

Saved Changes

Scroll to the Saved Changes card. Filter by member and month using the dropdowns. Tap Delete to remove an entry. Entries tagged PDF upload were saved automatically by the weekly roster upload — you can still delete or edit them if needed.
