Getting Started

Open the app, then pick your name from the dropdown at the top — your roster appears straight away. Nothing to install and no password needed just to view your shifts.

Each day shows a coloured badge for the shift you're on. Tap any day to see the full detail — start and end times, plus anything your manager has recorded.

Works Offline

Your base roster is saved on your device after the first load, so the calendar always opens — even with no signal. Shift changes recorded by your manager, and the Daily Huddle, need internet to load the latest.

The Buttons

🏖️ AL
Your annual leave: Remaining · Taken · Booked · Entitlement. Book leave through the normal Chiltern process — the app shows the balance.
📝 Admin
Staff self-service — record annual leave that's already been agreed, or an absence, and view your saved changes. Sign in with your name and password — your surname to start with, then you'll be asked to choose your own. Forgotten it? Speak to the admin.
💷 Pay
The Pay Calculator — estimate your take-home for any 4-week period. See the separate Pay Calculator Guide.
👥 Team
The whole team's shifts for any week. Switch CEA · CES · Dispatcher; use ← → to change week.
☰ Menu
Top-left. The Daily Huddle, Weekly Retail Circular, Marylebone Newsletter, guides, Settings and every page.

Turn on notifications for a new Huddle or pay reminder: ☰ → Settings → Enable notifications. On iPhone you must Add to Home Screen first and open it from there — notifications don't work from a browser tab.

Add Your Work Email

In ☰ Menu → ⚙ Settings, add your @chilternrailways.co.uk email in the 📧 Work Email card. It's for self-service password reset (in a future update) — worth adding now so it's ready. Adding it doesn't change how you sign in. Only you and the admin can see it. Settings is also where you turn notifications on or off and set your own password.

Signing In

  1. Tap 📝 Admin on the main screen
  2. Pick your name from the dropdown
  3. Enter your password — your surname by default (lowercase, no spaces or punctuation), or a password you've set yourself
  4. Tap Sign in — you stay signed in for 60 days, however often or rarely you open the app
Example: "D. Smith" → the default password is smith
Set your own password in ☰ Settings → 🔑 Password — it replaces the surname default with one only you know. You'll be asked to choose one the next time you sign in, so the surname default is on its way out. Forgotten it? The admin can reset it back to your surname — speak to the admin.

You can record leave and absence for yourself. Managers and the admin can also record changes for other staff.

Change a Shift

  1. Pick the staff member in the bar at the top
  2. Move to the week you need — swipe or use ‹ ›, or tap the date to jump
  3. On the day, tap a type: AL · Shift · RDW · Absent · Rest · Other
  4. The card highlights (gold edge) to show it's selected
  5. Enter Start / End times for types that need them
  6. Check the "You're about to save…" line, then tap Save changes
Already recorded? A day that already has a saved change opens with a ✓ Saved badge and a warm edge. Change it and it reads Updating; untick it and it reads Removing — nothing is written until you Save.

Apply to Several Days

Tick the boxes on several days to open the Apply to multiple days bar: pick the days (Mon–Fri, Working days, All 7), choose a type, and tap Apply to ticked days. For leave spanning several days, use the Record Annual Leave card and pick a date range instead.

Leave & Absence

The 🏖️ Record Annual Leave and 🪑 Record Absence cards each take a date range — rest days and Sundays are skipped automatically. The AL card shows the running balance as you go.

Saved Changes

The 📋 Saved Changes card lists everything recorded. Filter by staff member and month; tap a row to edit or delete. Rows tagged roster upload came from the weekly PDF — you can still edit or remove them.

The "Other" Family

Tap Other and a small menu appears — pick Training, Induction, Assessment, Team Day, Union or Meeting. These pay as the day underneath (the shift they replace), so times are optional. Tick Rest day (RDW) if it fell on a rest day.

📋 Spare also lives in the Other menu. It's a rarely-used placeholder for a standby day whose shift isn't set yet — it keeps its own purple badge and doesn't pay a premium.

Weekly Roster Upload Admins only

Admins upload the weekly roster PDF in Operations — an admin-only page most staff won't see. The app reads it and shows a review grouped by person: ticked rows will save, and where a day clashes with something already recorded it asks Keep saved or Use new roster. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Sundays

Sundays are uncontracted for every grade, so AL and Absent can't be recorded on them. A worked Sunday is always RDW (1.5×), never annual leave or absence.
