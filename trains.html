ℹ️ Good to know

Where these times come from, and what is not here yet
▾
  • Times at Marylebone only. Which stations each December train stops at is not on this page yet. A train with the same time can still stop at different stations.
  • December times can still change. Before you tell a customer, check the journey planner on the Chiltern Railways website.
  • Aylesbury has two routes. Via High Wycombe or via Amersham. A train that changes route is shown as going somewhere different, because the journey is.
  • Joined trains count once. Two trains coupled together leave as one.
