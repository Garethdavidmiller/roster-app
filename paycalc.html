To make up for this, they calculate a Holiday Pay Premium (HPP) and pay it as a lump sum every January. 📖 Pay Calculator Guide — what each figure means →
⚠️ Estimate only — not financial or tax advice. Tax is estimated by dividing your annual allowance equally across all 13 pay periods. If you enter Year to Date totals from your payslip, the app works out tax the way payroll does — adding up the whole year so far — usually closer to your real payslip, especially later in the year. Actual figures may still vary slightly due to employer rounding or payroll corrections. London Allowance is included automatically.
