🇪🇺 FIP Travel

European rail travel for Chiltern staff · July 2026

FIP is your European rail travel benefit as a Chiltern Railways employee — discounted or free travel on most European national rail networks. This page tells you what to do, where it works, and how to book. (FIP stands for the French Facilités de circulation Internationales du Personnel des chemins de fer — International Travel Facilities for Railway Staff.)

What do I need?

Free travelFIP coupons — valid on participating operators only
Discounted faresFIP Card — 50% on most; 75% on French, Belgian and Irish trains

What you get

🃏

FIP Card

Discounted fares on most European railways. The standard discount is 50%; French, Belgian and Irish trains offer 75% off. The card is valid for two calendar years plus a little more — from 1 December before the printed period to 31 January after it (a 2025–26 card runs 1 Dec 2024 to 31 Jan 2027). Buy tickets at station offices abroad, or book from the UK via Trainseurope or bookmyrst.co.uk.

🎟️

Free Coupons

Free travel on participating train operators. On some trains you'll also need to pay for a seat reservation or a supplement. Each coupon covers 48 hours in one country. Coupons are not sent automatically — order the ones you need each year from your annual allowance.

Who's covered You generally qualify after 12 months' service with a National Rail train operator (FIP is a discretionary benefit, not a contractual right). It then covers you, your partner (you must live together) and your dependent children. Whether family members get free coupons depends on the country — check the country cards below. Confirm with RST before ordering coupons for family members.
Family coupons — not every country Where family coupons aren't available, your partner and children can still travel at the FIP discount rate using the FIP Card.

How it works

  1. Apply for your FIP Card Use the Rail Staff Travel (RST) form — link at the bottom of this page. RST is the team, run by the Rail Delivery Group (RDG), that administers staff travel benefits on behalf of UK train operators. Allow at least 6 weeks. For your first card, apply ahead of time even without travel plans — applications take time. Once you hold a card, RST normally renews it automatically, so you don't re-apply (the per-trip coupons are the thing you order each time). When it arrives, sign it and check your name matches your passport.
  2. Order your free coupons once a year Use the RST coupon form (link at the bottom). Order at least 3 weeks before travel; they're usually posted about 2 weeks before. A coupon is valid for up to 3 months from the outward (start) date you declare — the start and end dates are printed on it (not simply 3 months from issue). As a rough guide you can order about one coupon per country per year, with two for a few (e.g. the Netherlands). But each operator sets its own annual limit, and family allowances often differ from staff — check the country cards below and confirm with RST before ordering.
  3. Understand how a coupon works Each coupon has 4 dated boxes. When you write a date in a box, you get 48 hours of free travel on participating operators in that country — from midnight at the start of that day through to midnight at the end of the next. So one coupon = up to 4 separate 48-hour trips in that country.
  4. Book your trip For most countries you can walk up to the ticket office once you arrive. To book ahead from the UK, your best options are Trainseurope (call 01354 660222 — you speak to a person and get it sorted) or bookmyrst.co.uk (online request form — allow at least 3 weeks, they email you a quote). See the "Booking from the UK" section below.
  5. Travel with card and passport Always carry both together. Staff at the ticket office and on board may ask to see them.
⚠ Filling in your coupons — permanent ink only Use a permanent ballpoint pen. Any crossing out or correction invalidates the coupon — it will be refused at the ticket office. Write carefully and check before you hand them over.
⚠ One date covers a full 48 hours A single dated box is valid for that whole day and all of the next — write Monday's date and you're covered for all of Monday and all of Tuesday. If a second box is marked for the same trip, politely show the printed validity and ask for it to be rechecked.

Booking from the UK

Four ways to book your FIP trip from the UK.

  1. Trainseurope Call 01354 660222 — Mon–Fri 09:00–19:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–15:00. Speak to a person and get it booked in one call. Handles most countries.
  2. SNCF direct Call 00 33 1 84 94 3635 — ask for the English option. Best for French domestic journeys on SNCF. They can email tickets to you. Not for Eurostar — use bookmyrst, Trainseurope, or St Pancras for that.
  3. bookmyrst.co.uk Online request form — submit your journey and they email a quote. Allow at least 3 weeks — no telephone bookings. Covers most routes.
  4. Ffestiniog Travel A specialist rail agency. Email rail@ffestiniogtravel.com — worth trying if Trainseurope can't help with your route.

Always compare public fares first A FIP discounted fare is not automatically the cheapest option. Advance public tickets, promotions, and low-cost operators can sometimes beat the FIP price — especially outside peak periods. Check before you book.
ℹ️ Travelling through more than one country? ▾

You need a coupon for each country you pass through — even if it's one continuous train.

Example: the Vienna–Venice overnight crosses from Austria into Italy at the border. You need one ÖBB (Austria) coupon and one Trenitalia (Italy) coupon for that single journey. The Belfast–Dublin Enterprise is the same — one NIR coupon (Dundalk to Belfast) and one Irish Rail coupon (Dublin to Dundalk), with Dundalk being the handover point between operators.

The exact changeover point between coupons is set per route. Trainseurope can tell you exactly which coupons you need before you order them.

Note: Eurostar and Eurostar Red don't accept coupons at all — fixed FIP fares apply instead (see sections above).

🇫🇷 France ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operator: SNCF (including TGV high-speed trains)

Show your FIP Card at an SNCF ticket office for 75% off most fares. Free coupons are also accepted. Reservations are compulsory on TGV and other long-distance trains. With the 75% FIP ticket the reservation is included in the price; if you're using a free coupon, a small reservation fee applies on top.

Cross-border TGV routes to Switzerland (operated by Lyria) don't accept coupons and use a fixed FIP price instead — see the Switzerland card for details. If in doubt on any other international route, check with Trainseurope before booking.

Domestic French journeys must be booked at an SNCF station ticket office or by phone — not online. From the UK, call the SNCF Contact Centre on 00 33 1 84 94 3635.

Getting around Paris The Paris Métro is not covered by FIP — buy a regular ticket or day pass. The RER is a mix: some lines are SNCF-operated (FIP valid) and some are RATP-operated (not valid). The simplest approach when you're in the city is to use a standard ticket — it's not worth the hassle of working out which section you're on.
⚠ OUIGO and Frecciarossa — FIP not valid OUIGO (low-cost TGV) won't accept FIP tickets or coupons. Frecciarossa trains from Milan to Paris, operated by Trenitalia, are also not covered by FIP in France — only SNCF-operated trains qualify.
Monaco Monaco is part of the French SNCF network — SNCF FIP tickets and coupons are valid on the coastal line that runs through Monaco towards Ventimiglia (Italy border).

🇧🇪 Belgium ⚠ buy before boarding from 1 July 2026▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operator: SNCB / NMBS

Both 75% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on SNCB services. No reservation is required — buy your ticket and travel. Note: the Diabolo surcharge applies to journeys to or from Brussels Airport (Zaventem) station even with FIP — a few euros each way; check the current amount when booking.

⚠ From 1 July 2026 — buy before boarding SNCB has announced it will no longer sell tickets on board, including FIP discounted tickets. Buy at a station ticket desk before you board. Confirm this is in effect before you travel in July 2026 or later.
⚠ TGV trains from France — SNCB coupons not valid SNCF TGV trains between Brussels and Lyon, Marseille, etc. don't accept SNCB coupons. A separate fixed-price FIP ticket must be booked (call Trainseurope). For Brussels–Paris, a Eurostar FIP fare is usually cheaper and easier.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇳🇱 Netherlands ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operator: NS

FIP tickets and coupons are bought at NS International ticket counters. Known locations include Amsterdam Centraal, Rotterdam Centraal, Schiphol Airport and Utrecht Centraal. Check which counters are open before you travel — availability can vary.

Ticket barriers Most Dutch stations have automatic ticket barriers. Coupon holders are issued a separate gate pass when they buy — use this at the barriers. FIP tickets include a QR code. If you can't open a gate, ask station staff.
Supplements: On a 50% FIP ticket a small per-journey supplement (about €3.20) applies on the Intercity Direct high-speed section between Schiphol and Rotterdam — a free coupon doesn't carry it. Amounts change, so check the exact charge with NS International when you buy.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇮🇪 Republic of Ireland & Northern Ireland ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Republic of Ireland: Iarnród Éireann (Irish Rail)
FIP gives 75% off or free travel (with coupons) on all Irish Rail mainline services. Buy tickets at Irish Rail station offices. No high-speed supplements — just the discounted fare. Reservations aren't mandatory but are recommended as trains can fill up.

Northern Ireland: Translink / NIR
FIP gives 75% off or free travel (with coupons) on NIR services. Buy tickets at Translink station offices.

Belfast ↔ Dublin — Enterprise service You need two coupons: one Irish Rail coupon (Dublin to Dundalk) and one NIR coupon (Dundalk to Belfast). You travel the whole journey on one train without changing — the coupons simply cover each operator's section. A FIP ticket covering the full route is also an option.
⚠ Luas (Dublin tram) — FIP not valid The Luas tram network in Dublin is not covered by FIP. Buy a standard ticket at any tram stop.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇩🇪 Germany ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: DB (Deutsche Bahn)

DB covers Germany's main rail network — ICE high-speed trains are included, with no domestic supplement on a FIP ticket or a free coupon. Reservations are optional (worth booking on busy long-distance trains). Buy at any DB station ticket office or book ahead at bahn.de. Free coupons are valid for staff. Cross-border routes such as Paris–Frankfurt (TGV/ICE) can also be booked at DB ticket offices.

⚠ Private operators — check before you travel Germany has over 80 train operators, most of which do not accept FIP. Many regional routes are run by private companies rather than DB, and they look identical in journey planners. FlixTrain and WESTbahn also don't accept FIP. Always confirm you're on a DB service before assuming FIP applies.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇦🇹 Austria ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operators: ÖBB and GySEV/Raaberbahn

ÖBB covers the vast majority of Austria's rail network, including the Railjet high-speed service. A 50% FIP ticket costs half the standard fare with no additional charge. Free coupons are also valid, but a surcharge applies on long-distance trains: €10 in 2nd class, €15 in 1st class. GySEV/Raaberbahn also accepts FIP — mainly relevant for cross-border journeys towards Hungary. ÖBB FIP tickets are also valid in Liechtenstein, where ÖBB operates the only railway line. Buy at any ÖBB or GySEV ticket office.

Railjet class: A second-class coupon covers Economy class; first-class coupons are needed for Railjet first class. Business class on Railjet isn't bookable with FIP at all.
⚠ Private operators — FIP not valid Westbahn and RegioJet run long-distance trains in Austria but don't accept FIP (they appear as IC or WB in journey planners). The CAT (City Airport Train Vienna) is also not in FIP — use an ÖBB suburban train to the airport instead.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇮🇹 Italy ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operator: Trenitalia

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are accepted at Trenitalia ticket offices. Frecciarossa, Frecciargento and Frecciabianca high-speed trains (and EC / EN / IC / ICN services) need a compulsory seat reservation and supplement: on a 50% FIP ticket both are included in the fare, while on a free coupon you must buy the reservation and the applicable supplement separately before you travel. Sicily is also reachable by Trenitalia night and InterCity trains, which include a ferry crossing of the Strait of Messina.

⚠ Private and regional operators — FIP not valid Italo high-speed trains don't accept FIP. Trenord — which runs regional trains across Lombardy (Milan, Lake Como, Bergamo) — also doesn't accept FIP despite sharing tracks with Trenitalia. Ferrovie Sud Est (Puglia/Bari area) is the same. Always check the operator before boarding a regional train in northern or southern Italy.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇪🇸 Spain ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: Renfe

Staff can use free coupons on standard Renfe services. On AVE high-speed trains a mandatory fixed staff supplement applies rather than a straight 50% off — check the current amount when you book. It's usually bought at a Renfe ticket office in Spain, though it can sometimes be arranged from the UK ahead of travel — ask Trainseurope or bookmyrst.co.uk. Most stations have ticket barriers; coupon holders need to ask staff to open them manually. For long-distance trains, arrive at least 30 minutes before departure — luggage checks are conducted at the station.

Euskotren (Basque Country) — free travel Euskotren is a separate rail network in the Basque Country (Bilbao and San Sebastián) that has joined FIP — travel is free, and staff can also use coupons. Verify current terms with RDG or Trainseurope before you go, as newer FIP participants can have changing arrangements.
⚠ Ouigo Spain, Iryo, and Avlo — FIP not valid Low-cost and private operators don't accept FIP: Ouigo Spain, Iryo, and Renfe's own low-cost brand Avlo. These all share high-speed lines with Renfe — check the operator before booking.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇨🇭 Switzerland ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operators: SBB (Swiss Federal Railways), BLS, and most Swiss private railways

FIP covers almost the entire Swiss rail network — SBB, BLS, and a consortium of private railways all take part. FIP gives 50% off throughout the network; free coupons are valid for staff. Buy at any SBB or BLS station ticket counter, online at sbb.ch, or from the UK via Trainseurope.

Mountain tourist railways — check first Some scenic mountain railways and cable cars (mainly around Grindelwald and Mürren) are not in FIP. If you're planning a specific mountain route, confirm with RDG or Trainseurope before you go.
Travelling to Italy — supplement applies Cross-border EuroCity (EC/ECE) trains between Switzerland and Milan need a mandatory seat reservation that acts as your supplement — about €20 per journey (the same in 1st and 2nd class), bought from SBB, at the border, or on board. Booking the same reservation through DB (German railways) is cheaper — roughly €10 second class / €13 first class, reservation included. Amounts change, so check when you book.
⚠ Paris–Switzerland TGV (Lyria) — coupons not valid These cross-border high-speed trains use a special FIP fare rather than 50% off, and free coupons are not accepted. Book via Trainseurope or SNCF direct.

✓ Checked Jul 2026 — carrier rules change; reconfirm with RDG before you travel.

🇵🇹 Portugal ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: CP (Comboios de Portugal)

FIP tickets (50% off) and free coupons are valid on CP services. Coupons are for staff only — family members travel on a 50% FIP ticket. Buy at any CP station ticket desk, or book from the UK via Trainseurope. Covers all main routes including Lisbon–Porto and Lisbon–Faro.

On long-distance AP (Alfa Pendular) and IC (Intercidades) trains, a seat reservation is required — this is booked alongside your FIP ticket at the ticket desk.

Vigo–Porto (Celta service) The cross-border Celta train between Vigo (Spain) and Porto uses a special FIP fare for the entire route. Ask at the ticket office — both a CP coupon and Renfe ticket together don't cover it; the fixed Celta FIP fare does.
⚠ Fertagus — FIP not valid Fertagus runs the R line from Lisbon (Roma-Areeiro) south towards Setúbal and is a private operator not in FIP. If you're travelling in the Lisbon area and see a train labelled "Setúbal" on Google Maps, check it's a CP service before assuming FIP applies.
🇱🇺 Luxembourg — free for everyone ▾
Free for all

Operator: CFL

Luxembourg is unique — domestic travel in second class is completely free for everyone. Trains, trams and buses. You don't need a ticket or your FIP Card to travel in second class — just board and go.

First class: A free coupon or 50% FIP ticket is required for first class. Second class is always free.
TGV trains from/to France — special fixed fare required SNCF's TGV services between Paris and Luxembourg cannot be used with a standard CFL FIP coupon. A separate fixed-price FIP ticket must be booked instead (call Trainseurope) — and these can be expensive. Regional trains to/from France are fine with normal coupons.
🇩🇰 Denmark ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: DSB

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on DSB main-line trains — coupons are for staff only. Buy at DSB ticket offices. The Hamburg–Copenhagen EC is a popular route running several times daily — book ahead as it fills up.

Branch lines — private operators Some branch lines run by GoCollective, Lokaltog, and other private operators are not in FIP. Stick to the DSB network or check before you travel.
⚠ Night trains and Copenhagen–Sweden — FIP not valid Night trains passing through Denmark (SJ and Snälltåget) don't accept FIP. Trains from Copenhagen towards Sweden (Malmö and beyond) use the Øresund bridge via Øresundståg — also not in FIP. You'll need a regular ticket for both.
🇳🇴 Norway ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operators: Vy Tog, SJ Nord, and Go-Ahead Nordic

FIP covers all three operators running Norway's passenger rail network. Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid — coupons for staff only. The Vy name is printed on FIP Cards and coupons, but all three operators accept them. Buy tickets at Vy ticket offices or use the Entur app (covers all Norwegian public transport) to check timetables.

⚠ Airport Express (Flytoget) — FIP not valid The dedicated airport express to Oslo Gardermoen is a private operator and does not accept FIP. Use a regular ticket, or travel on the slower but valid Vy service to the airport instead.
⚠ Flåm Railway — FIP not valid The famous Flåm Railway (one of Norway's most popular tourist trips) is not in FIP. Pay standard public fares.
🇨🇿 Czech Republic ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: České dráhy (ČD)

FIP covers most ČD routes, but not all — a small number of ČD services are exceptions, and some trains carry a supplement. When using coupons, verify your specific journey before you go. When buying a FIP ticket at the ČD desk, staff will only issue it for valid trains, so that's the safer option. Buy at ČD ticket offices. Prague is well connected to Vienna, Berlin, and Budapest — cross-border journeys need a FIP ticket from each country's operator.

⚠ Private operators — FIP not valid Several private operators share Czech routes but don't accept FIP: RegioJet, Leo Express, ARRIVA vlaky, GW Train Regio, and Trilex/Die Länderbahn. Stick to ČD trains — and check before you travel, as private operators run alongside ČD on many corridors.
🇭🇺 Hungary ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: MÁV (Hungarian State Railways)

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on MÁV services — coupons for staff only. Buy at MÁV ticket offices. Budapest is the main hub and well connected to Vienna by Railjet (around 2h 40min). Coupons follow the country you're travelling through, not the operator: even though the Railjet is ÖBB-operated, the Hungarian section of a Vienna–Budapest trip needs a Hungarian (MÁV) coupon and the Austrian section an ÖBB coupon — or use a single through FIP-discounted ticket for the whole journey.

🇭🇷 Croatia ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: HŽ (Croatian Railways)

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on HŽ services — coupons for staff only. Buy at HŽ ticket offices on arrival, or book from the UK via Trainseurope (route options are limited).

Limited coastal rail network Most popular Croatian destinations — Dubrovnik, Split, Hvar — are not well served by rail. Zagreb is the main hub and useful for connections to Ljubljana and Budapest, but reaching the coast requires a bus or ferry from Zagreb.
🇬🇷 Greece ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff + family ✓

Operator: Hellenic Train

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on Hellenic Train services, including long-distance, regional, and suburban (Proastiakos) trains. Buy at Hellenic Train ticket offices. The main intercity route is Athens–Thessaloniki. Attica Group ferries between Italy and Greece also accept FIP — see the ferry section below.

Getting around Athens and Thessaloniki City metro systems in both Athens and Thessaloniki are run by separate companies and are not covered by FIP — buy a standard metro ticket for travel within either city.
🇵🇱 Poland ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only KMŁ new April 2026

Operators: PKP and multiple regional railways

Poland has more FIP operators than almost any other country. PKP covers national long-distance and much of regional traffic. Several regional railways also participate: KD (Lower Silesia), KMŁ (Małopolska / Kraków area, joined April 2026), KŚ (Silesia), KW (Greater Poland), and ŁKA (Łódź area). Buy at the relevant operator's ticket office. On PKP InterCity long-distance trains, reservations are sometimes mandatory and generally recommended — trains fill up quickly.

⚠ SKM Warsaw (city rail) — FIP not valid The Szybka Kolej Miejska (SKM) commuter rail network in Warsaw is not in FIP. Buy a standard city ticket for travel within Warsaw.
🇸🇰 Slovakia ▾
FIP Card ✓ Coupons: staff only

Operator: ZSSK (Slovak Railways)

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on ZSSK services — coupons for staff only. Connects to Vienna, Budapest and Prague. Buy at ZSSK ticket offices. Ask about reservations when you buy — they're required on some trains.

High Tatras mountain railway The Tatranská železnica electric mountain railway in the High Tatras is operated by ZSSK and is covered by FIP — a rare chance to take a mountain railway included on your FIP ticket.
⚠ LeoExpress and RegioJet — FIP not valid Both private operators run trains in Slovakia but do not accept FIP. They appear alongside ZSSK services in journey planners — check the operator before assuming FIP applies.

Operator: CFR Călători

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on CFR Călători services — coupons for staff only. Buy at ticket offices. Trains are slow — build in extra time.

Both 50% FIP tickets and free coupons are valid on BDŽ services — coupons for staff only. Buy at ticket offices — online booking is very limited.

Ferry services

A small number of ferry operators take part in FIP — useful for reaching Ireland or Greece, whether you're travelling as a foot passenger or taking a car.

⛴️ Stena Line — North Sea (Harwich–Hook of Holland) ▾
Coupons: staff + family ✓

Route: Harwich → Hook of Holland (Netherlands)

Free travel with coupons — you need two coupons per person (staff and each eligible family member). A Port Tax and Environmental Charge is payable separately — not covered by the coupon.

You must book in advance — tickets cannot be bought on board. Call Stena on 01255 202352 and have your coupon number(s) ready before you call.

Overnight crossing: A cabin must be booked — it's not optional. Do this at the same time as booking your ticket.

Always check RDG before booking — ferry FIP details can change.

⛴️ Stena Line — Irish Sea ▾
FIP Card: 50% at port only No coupons

Routes: Holyhead → Dublin  |  Fishguard → Rosslare  |  Cairnryan → Belfast

Coupons are not accepted on Irish Sea Stena routes.

⚠ No advance booking from the UK on a FIP fare The 50% FIP discount is only available in person at the port ticket office on the day of travel. You cannot pre-book a FIP-price ticket from home — you must turn up at the port and buy it there.

Always check RDG before booking — ferry FIP details can change.

⛴️ Superfast Ferries — Italy to Greece ▾
Fixed fare — no coupons

Routes: Ancona / Bari → Patras, Igoumenitsa, Corfu

Superfast Ferries (operated by Attica Group) use a fixed FIP price — not a percentage discount and not standard coupons. You need to book by sending the Attica FIP Reservation Form by email before you travel. The current booking contact and form are available on the full RDG FIP guide (link at the bottom of this page).

Common mistakes

  • Assuming Eurostar accepts FIP coupons — it doesn't. Fixed FIP fares apply instead
  • Assuming FIP is always cheaper than public fares — compare advance tickets first
  • Not booking FIP seats early — they're limited and sell out weeks ahead on popular routes
  • Writing on a coupon too early — fill in the date as close to travel as possible, in permanent ink, no corrections
  • Using coupons on private or low-cost operators (Italo, OUIGO, Ouigo Spain, RegioJet) — these don't accept FIP
  • Assuming city metros and trams are included — they almost never are
  • Forgetting that reservations are still required on many high-speed trains even with a coupon
  • Trying to buy on board in Belgium from July 2026 — you must buy before boarding

What to carry

  • Passport
  • FIP Card (if using discounted fares)
  • FIP coupons filled in correctly (if using free travel)
  • Seat reservation or supplement confirmation where required
  • Printed or digital ticket confirmation
  • Any booking reference from Trainseurope / bookmyrst.co.uk

Important to know

  • Leisure travel only — not for commuting or any work purpose
  • Always carry your FIP Card and passport together
  • Book early — FIP seats are limited and sell out fast on popular trains
  • Eligible for you, your spouse/partner, and dependent children
  • Participating operators only — city metros, trams, buses and private rail operators are generally not in FIP. You'll need a regular ticket to get around each city
  • Coupons match your UK staff travel pass class — if you have a standard (second-class) pass, your coupons are for second class
  • FIP is not a contractual entitlement — it can change or be withdrawn at any time
  • Normal delay and cancellation terms apply — travel is at your own risk
