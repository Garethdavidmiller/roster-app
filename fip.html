🇪🇺 FIP Travel

European rail travel for Chiltern staff · July 2026

FIP is your European rail travel benefit as a Chiltern Railways employee — discounted or free travel on most European national rail networks. This page tells you what to do, where it works, and how to book. (FIP stands for the French Facilités de circulation Internationales du Personnel des chemins de fer — International Travel Facilities for Railway Staff.)

What do I need?

Free travelFIP coupons — valid on participating operators only
Discounted faresFIP Card — 50% on most; 75% on French, Belgian and Irish trains
EurostarSpecial FIP fare — coupons are not accepted
Metros / tramsRegular local ticket — FIP rarely covers city transport
Family / partnerUsually covered — coupon eligibility varies by country (see below)

What you get

🃏

FIP Card

Discounted fares on most European railways. The standard discount is 50%; French, Belgian and Irish trains offer 75% off. The card is valid for two calendar years plus a little more — from 1 December before the printed period to 31 January after it (a 2025–26 card runs 1 Dec 2024 to 31 Jan 2027). Buy tickets at station offices abroad, or from the UK through bookmyrst.co.uk. It is personal, must be signed, cannot be transferred, and must be shown with your passport — the names must match.

🎟️

Free Coupons

Free travel on participating train operators. On some trains you'll also need to pay for a seat reservation or a supplement. A coupon is issued for a named railway or shipping company — not simply for a country — and some countries have more than one. Coupons are not sent automatically — order the ones you need each year from your annual allowance.

Who's covered You generally qualify after 12 months' service with a National Rail train operator (FIP is a discretionary benefit, not a contractual right). It then covers you, your partner (you must live together) and your dependent children. Whether family members get free coupons depends on the country — check the country cards below. Confirm with RST before ordering coupons for family members.
Family coupons — not every country Where family coupons aren't available, your partner and children can still travel at the FIP discount rate using the FIP Card.
⚠ The FIP Card does not discount a child fare It discounts the full adult fare, and a child travelling on it gets that discounted adult fare — which may or may not be cheaper than the ordinary child fare. Compare the two before you buy. Age limits for child fares are set by each operator for domestic travel; internationally, children under 4 usually travel free (unless a reservation is required) and up to 12 usually get a discount on the adult fare.

How it works

  1. Apply for your FIP Card Use the Rail Staff Travel (RST) form — link at the bottom of this page. RST is the team, run by the Rail Delivery Group (RDG), that administers staff travel benefits on behalf of UK train operators. Allow at least 6 weeks. For your first card, apply ahead of time even without travel plans — applications take time. Once you hold a card, RST normally renews it automatically, so you don't re-apply (the per-trip coupons are the thing you order each time). When it arrives, sign it and check your name matches your passport.
  2. Request coupons from your annual allowance Use the RST coupon form (link at the bottom). Order the coupons you need for a planned trip — you don't have to request a whole year's worth at once. Allow at least 3 weeks before travel; they're usually posted about 2 weeks before. A coupon is valid for up to 3 months from the outward (start) date you declare — the start and end dates are printed on it (not simply 3 months from issue). Your allowance is set separately for each participating company, and it varies: some give one coupon a year, some two, some include eligible family and some are staff-only — and two companies in the same country can differ. There is no reliable "one per country" rule of thumb. Check the country cards below against the current RST allocation table, and confirm with RST before ordering.
  3. Understand how a coupon works A rail coupon normally has 4 dated boxes. Writing a date in one box gives free travel on that company's eligible services from 00:01 on that date until midnight at the end of the following day. That is a validity period, not a single trip — you can make as many eligible journeys as you like within it. So one coupon gives you up to 4 such periods on that company's services.
  4. Book your trip For most countries you can walk up to the ticket office once you arrive. To book ahead from the UK, your best options are Trainseurope (call 01354 660222 — you speak to a person and get it sorted) or bookmyrst.co.uk (online request form — allow at least 3 weeks, they email you a quote). See the "Booking from the UK" section below.
  5. Travel with card and passport Always carry both together. Staff at the ticket office and on board may ask to see them.
⚠ Filling in your coupons — permanent ink only Use a permanent ballpoint pen and fill the boxes in order, writing the date before you board. Never use pencil or an erasable pen — that is a travel irregularity and can cost you your travel facilities permanently.

If you make a mistake, you lose that one box — not the coupon. You may not alter or write over a date. Block out the error, write the correct date in the next box, and carry on using the coupon. The boxes you have not used are unaffected.
⚠ One dated box covers today and all of tomorrow A dated box runs until midnight the following day — date it on Monday and you are covered for Monday and all of Tuesday, however many journeys you make. It never runs past the coupon's printed expiry.

Rail Staff Travel's two pages differ on when the day starts: the FIP page says 00:01, the Conditions of Issue and Use say from the moment you write the date. Date the box before your first journey and the difference cannot affect you.

Booking from the UK

Five ways to book your FIP trip. bookmyrst.co.uk is Rail Staff Travel's own partner, and France domestic can only be done by SNCF direct — otherwise pick whichever suits the route.

  1. Trainseurope Call 01354 660222 — Mon–Fri 09:00–19:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–15:00. Speak to a person and get it booked in one call. Handles most countries. Not named by Rail Staff Travel, but the quickest route to a human.
  2. bookmyrst.co.uk — the RST partner RST work with International Rail, a travel agent, for a fixed transaction fee. Submit your journey online and they prepare a quote and contact you with the price. Allow at least 3 weeks — no telephone bookings. Also covers Eurostar and Thalys. What they can and cannot reach country by country is in the table below.
  3. SNCF direct — the only way to book French domestic RST state this as an exception: domestic French journeys can only be booked through the SNCF Contact Centre. Call the English-speaking SNCF Voyageurs line on +33 1 84 94 36 35; at the end of the French menu press #85 for an English operator, then 1 for Voyages and 1 again for TGV. Have your dates, passenger count, whether each is on a coupon or a FIP Card, and your origin and destination ready. You get a booking reference and eTicket by email — carry it with your coupon or card. Not for Eurostar — use bookmyrst, Trainseurope or St Pancras for that.
  4. Ffestiniog Travel A specialist rail agency. Email rail@ffestiniogtravel.com — worth trying if Trainseurope can't help with your route. Also not named by RST.
  5. A ticket office in the country itself Always available, and the fallback everywhere the table below says no. Most operators do not allow buying on board unless you boarded at an unstaffed station — and some prohibit boarding without a ticket even where there was no ticket office. The country cards say which. Machines, operator apps and websites sometimes work too, but not always for FIP — see Czechia and Slovakia for two that look like they do and don't.
⚠ SNCF takes Visa or American Express — not Mastercard The SNCF Contact Centre accepts Visa, Eurocard or American Express only. Mastercard and other cards are refused, and SNCF say the accepted list can change without notice — say which card you intend to use before the agent starts the transaction.
💡 If a French ticket office clerk doesn't know the FIP fare code SNCF publish them, and quoting one saves the argument. Active staff and family: coupon reservation CF70; coupon reservation upgraded to 1st class CF71; FIP Card 75% ticket with compulsory reservation CF77; that ticket upgraded to 1st class CF78. (Retired staff use CF52 and CF54.)

ℹ️ Travelling through more than one country? ▾

You need the right company's coupon for each country or railway section you pass through — even if it's one continuous train. The branding of the through train does not matter: one operator's coupon is not valid over another company's territory, and some countries have several separate FIP companies, so a coupon "for that country" may not cover every operator in it.

Example: the Vienna–Venice overnight crosses from Austria into Italy at the border. You need one ÖBB (Austria) coupon and one Trenitalia (Italy) coupon for that single journey. The Belfast–Dublin Enterprise is the same — one NIR coupon (Dundalk to Belfast) and one Irish Rail coupon (Dublin to Dundalk), with Dundalk being the handover point between operators.

The exact changeover point between coupons is set per route. Trainseurope can tell you exactly which coupons you need before you order them.

Note: Eurostar and Eurostar Red don't accept coupons at all — fixed FIP fares apply instead (see sections above).

🇫🇷 France ▾
FIP Card 75% Coupons: staff + family ✓ TGV: reservation + supplement

Operator: SNCF. Two coupons a year for you and two for each dependant, 75% on the FIP Card. Valid on TGV inOui and on TER regional trains — though note some TER services are run by other operators, and local authorities set their own fare conditions, so ask at the ticket office.

⚠ TGV and most Intercités Seat reservations are compulsory and a supplement is payable. Book ahead — FIP quotas are limited.

Buy before boarding — FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought on the train. Upgrading 2nd to 1st by paying the fare difference is done at a station.

Global fares apply on Lyria and on services between Belgium and the south of France.

FIP is not valid on SNCF coach services.

NOMAD routes: tickets must be validated in the platform machine (composteur) before each outward and return journey and after a break — but this does not apply to FIP coupons.

🇧🇪 Belgium ▾
FIP Card 75% Coupons: staff + family ✓ Buy before boarding

Operator: SNCB/NMBS. Two coupons a year for you and two for each dependant, 75% on the FIP Card. Coupons and discounted tickets are valid without restriction on SNCB's own services.

⚠ You cannot buy a ticket on the train Tickets must be bought before boarding, on every service. There is no on-train purchase.
⚠ A supplement can apply even to a stamped coupon On certain SNCB services a supplement is payable by coupon holders even where the coupon is endorsed "No Supplement Necessary", and by FIP Card ticket holders.

Brussels – Rotterdam – Amsterdam: on both Eurocity Direct (ECD) and Eurocity (EC) the NS supplement applies between Rotterdam and Schiphol. On discounted tickets the ECD and EC prices differ and are not interchangeable — a ticket for one is not valid on the other.

🇳🇱 Netherlands ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff + family ✓ Coupons dodge the supplements

Operator: NS. Two coupons a year for you and two for each dependant, 50% on the FIP Card.

💡 Here, a coupon is worth more than the card Supplements are payable on FIP discounted tickets — but no supplement is payable when you travel on a free coupon. That applies to the Intercity Direct supplement and to international 'D' express trains (where a discounted-ticket supplement can be settled on the train).

Brussels – Rotterdam – Amsterdam: on Eurocity Direct (ECD) and Eurocity (EC) the NS supplement applies between Rotterdam and Schiphol. Discounted-ticket prices for ECD and EC differ and are not interchangeable.

Buy before boarding — FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought on the train.

🇮🇪 Ireland & Northern Ireland ▾
FIP Card 75% Coupons: staff + family ✓ Two operators, two coupons

Two separate FIP undertakings — Iarnród Éireann (CIÉ) in the Republic and NIR in Northern Ireland. Each gives two coupons a year for you and two for each dependant, at 75% on the FIP Card.

⚠ The Enterprise (Dublin–Belfast) needs BOTH coupons To travel free on the cross-border Enterprise you must hold a CIÉ coupon and an NIR coupon. One will not do — the train crosses between two undertakings.

Coupons must not be used in Citygold (peak Dublin–Cork).

Upgrading: on 2nd class FIP you can travel 1st by paying the Superstandard supplement — at some large stations before departure, otherwise on the train. With a 1st class coupon you only pay a supplement where a reservation is required. Pay at irishrail.ie using the Seat Reservation button, labelled "Free Travel Pass or Valid Ticket".

Breaking your journey: a FIP reduced-rate ticket must be endorsed at the station where you break.

In Northern Ireland FIP is valid on NIR long-distance and suburban services, and the FIP Card can buy reduced-rate tickets at a station or on board. Not valid on buses.

🇩🇪 Germany ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff only No supplement on ICE/EC/IC

Operator: DB. One coupon a year, employee only, 50% on the FIP Card.

💡 No supplement on the fast trains Coupon holders pay no supplement on ICE, EC or IC services within Germany. Germany is one of the easiest countries to use a coupon in.
⚠ Buy before boarding — no exceptions, and the penalty is steep FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought on board, even if the station you boarded at had no open ticket office. Travelling without one costs double the fare for the distance already covered (minimum €60) plus the flexible fare to your destination.
⚠ Two ways to buy the wrong ticket Ticket machines sell 50% tickets for German schemes such as Bahncard — those are not valid with a FIP Card. And in a Verkehrsverbund (regional combined-fares area), FIP coupons and reduced-rate tickets are not valid for a journey lying entirely inside the zone.

Seats: one marked "ggf. reserviert" is held for late reservations — DB advise not to take it. When planning, check the Auslastungsanzeige loading indicator on bahn.de or in DB Navigator; DB ask staff travellers to avoid the busiest trains.

✓ Checked Aug 2026 against Rail Staff Travel

🇦🇹 Austria & Liechtenstein ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff + family ✓ Supplement on fast trains

Operator: ÖBB. One coupon a year for you and one for each dependant.

⚠ Fast trains carry a supplement On IC, ICE, EC, RJX, RJ and D trains a supplement of €15 first class / €10 second class is payable. It lasts as long as the dated box on your coupon — so up to 48 hours, not just one train.

Coupons stamped "No Supplement Necessary" are exempt — except on RJX and RJ within Austria, and on certain routes.

Journeys to or from Italy always need the FIP supplement, even with a "No supplement required" stamp.

Wholly within Austria (say Vienna–Klagenfurt) an ÖBB supplement of €10 second / €15 first applies, and this one can be paid on the train.

Buy before boarding — on-board tickets cost more, and on suburban routes buying before you board is mandatory.

Seat labels: a seat marked "ggf. reserviert" is being held for last-minute reservations. ÖBB's advice is not to sit in it. Business compartments are for Senior Manager Free Pass holders only.

✓ Checked Aug 2026 against Rail Staff Travel

🇮🇹 Italy ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff + family ✓ Long distance: reserve first

Operator: FS / Trenitalia. One coupon a year for you and one for each dependant, 50% on the FIP Card. Includes Trenitalia TPER local services in Emilia-Romagna.

Coupon or card? On long distance they work differently On Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Frecciabianca, EC, EN, IC and ICN:

With a coupon — a seat reservation is compulsory and supplements are payable on top.
With a FIP discounted ticket — the reservation and supplements are included in the fare.
⚠ Sort supplements out before you start If supplements or boarding fees are not bought before the journey begins, an extra on-train fee is charged — unless the station you left had no booking office or machine.

Regional trains are simple: no restriction and no supplement for coupons on R and RV services.

🇪🇸 Spain ▾
RENFE: FIP Card 50% Euskotren: 100% ✓ Coupons: staff only High-speed: coupon ≠ free

Two FIP undertakings. RENFE — the national network, one coupon a year, FIP Card 50%. And Euskotren Trena (Basque Railways) — one coupon a year and, unusually, a 100% FIP Card discount. Not valid on Euskotren buses, or on the Railway Museum tourist train at Azpeitia.

⚠ A coupon does not give free travel on Spanish high-speed On AVE, Alvia, Euromed, Intercity and Avant, a coupon does not cover the journey — you must buy a fixed supplement. Coupons give genuinely free travel on MD trains that do not require a reservation.
⚠ Reservations cannot be bought online They are mandatory on long-distance and medium-distance trains, can only be booked at a station ticket office, and you may be refused access to the platform without one. Most MD trains in Catalonia are non-reservable; most elsewhere are not — check before you board.

Reservation fees (April 2026): AVE and AVE International within Spain — Elige €10.00, Elige Confort €13.00, Premium €23.50.

Sleepers and class: supplements apply on IC, Talgo, Trainhotel and Euromed. Luxury Class or Shower Car (Cama Gran Clase) needs the full sleeping-car supplement. On 2nd class FIP you cannot upgrade to a 1st class discounted ticket, but you may book Cama Preferente or Gran Clase by paying the difference between the public 2nd and 1st class fares.

FIP is valid on RENFE's metre-gauge (formerly FEVE) services.

🇨🇭 Switzerland ▾
FIP Card 50% SBB coupons: staff only BLS coupons: staff + family ✓ SP coupons: staff only

Three separate FIP undertakings, with different coupon entitlements: SBB (Swiss Federal Railways, staff only), BLS (around Bern — staff and eligible family, so don't assume a partner has no Swiss coupon) and SP — Schweizer Privatbahnen, the Swiss private railways group (staff only), which covers many smaller operators.

⚠ Buy before you board — every carrier FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought on a train or ship. Travelling without one gets you a penalty fare. At some stations you must also stamp the ticket in the platform validator before boarding.

Buying: online at the SBB portal or at a ticket machine — choose the Half Fare option to get the 50%.

⚠ The urban zone-ticket trap Ask for a station-to-station ticket in a city and you may be sold a zonal ticket that also covers trams and buses outside FIP. It is valid for Swiss Half Fare cardholders — but with a FIP Card you may only use it on FIP-scheme companies.

BLS covers all BLS rail and bus services (including Busland AG) plus BLS boats on Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. Not valid on funiculars or cable cars, or on car-carrying trains through the Simplon and Lötschberg tunnels.

SBB buses are not in FIP unless they are a temporary rail replacement.

🏔️ "Mountain railway" is not a validity rule Many famous mountain lines are in FIP through the SP group — but each has its own rule, so check the line, not the category:

Glacier Express (RhB + MGB) — coupons and discounted fares both work, but reservations are compulsory and sell out weeks ahead. Book via International Rail. Ordinary RhB trains on the same St Moritz–Chur–Andermatt–Brig–Zermatt route need no reservation — the cheap way to see it.

Bernina Express (RhB, Chur–Tirano) — same picture: reservation and supplement required, and unbranded RhB services on the same route need neither.

Zermatt–Gornergrat (MGB/GGB) — discounted fares only; coupons are not accepted.

Jungfrau area (BGF, BLM, BOB, HB, JB, WAB) — some routes take coupons, others are discount-only, including Kleine Scheidegg–Jungfraujoch.

Treno Panoramico Vigezzo Vision (FART/SSIF, Domodossola–Locarno) — coupons and fares, reservation compulsory.

Pilatus Railway is not in FIP at all.
Travelling to Italy, or on a TGV — supplement applies On EuroCity trains between Switzerland and Italy, and on cross-border TGV services, holders of free coupons and FIP discounted tickets must pay the full supplement. Seat reservations are mandatory on EC trains (that is what the supplement pays for) and on all TGV trains.

Rail Staff Travel states the requirement but not a price, so no figure is quoted here — ask when you book.
⚠ Paris–Switzerland TGV (Lyria) — coupons not valid These cross-border high-speed trains use a special FIP fare rather than 50% off, and free coupons are not accepted. Book via Trainseurope or SNCF direct.

🇳🇴 Norway ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff only Supplements vary by operator

Vy is the FIP undertaking — one coupon a year, employee only — but FIP is valid on the intercity trains of Vy, SJ (SJ Nord) and Go-Ahead Nordic alike, and on any franchised operator that wins a Norwegian Traffic Package. The name on your coupon is Vy; the train may say something else.

Coupons are valid on Vy group Regiontog and Lokaltog services in the Oslo area, and on Vy local trains to and from the airport — except the long-distance Trondheim service, which only sets down there.

⚠ Flytoget Airport Express — not FIP The dedicated Oslo Airport express is outside FIP. Use a Vy local train instead, which is covered.
⚠ City fare zones are excluded FIP discounted tickets are not available inside the Skyss (Bergen), AtB (Trondheim) or Kolumbus (Stavanger) fare areas.

Supplements differ by operator — Go-Ahead Nordic, SJ Norge and Vy Tog each set their own, and some services carry mandatory seat reservations. SJ's seat supplement is 50 NOK. Entur runs the ticket retailing and can tell you what each supplement includes.

🇨🇿 Czech Republic ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff only Leisure coupons pay a supplement

Operator: České dráhy (ČD). One coupon a year, employee only, 50% on the FIP Card.

⚠ The one country where a leisure coupon costs you money On trains ČD classifies as "commercial" a supplement of about €6 is payable — but only when travelling on a FIP leisure coupon. It is not charged on a FIP duty coupon, and not on a ticket bought with a FIP Card. It covers 48 hours (00:01 on the first day to midnight the next), is issued per traveller against your coupon number and is non-transferable. Buy it up to 60 days ahead at a ČD ticket office, a UNIPOK 2G counter, the ČD eShop, or on board where staff have the equipment.
⚠ The 50% in the ČD app is not FIP FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought from the ČD app — the app's own 50% discount comes from a different quota. Buy at a ticket office. On board, you may only buy where you started at a station whose ticket office was closed.

Reservations: a seat reservation is needed with a FIP discounted ticket on SC services.

Breaking your journey is not permitted on a domestic ticket for up to 100 km. At 101 km or more it is — except on trains passing through Germany, where you may not leave or join the train either.

FIP is not available in Business Class on RailJet (not the same thing as RegioJet), on ČD cable cars, on special and historic trains, or on ČD services run under a public tender ČD won — ČD have not said which those are.

Buses: coupons are valid on no ČD bus. FIP discounted tickets work on just two — Václav Havel Airport ↔ Praha hl. n. and Hranice na Moravě ↔ Frýdek-Místek — and you must buy, with a compulsory reservation, at least 15 minutes before boarding.

⚠ Other operators — FIP not valid RegioJet, Leo Express, GW Train Regio, Desná Railway (Arriva Morava) and JHMD run alongside ČD on many corridors. Check the operator before assuming FIP applies.

🇭🇺 Hungary ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff only Two operators, two coupons

Two separate FIP undertakings. MÁV (Hungarian State Railways) covers most of the country. GySEV (Raaberbahn) runs western Hungary and part of south-east Austria. Each gives one coupon a year, employee only, at 50% on the FIP Card — and a MÁV coupon is not a GySEV coupon.

⚠ Cross-border trains: check the endorsement box on your coupon On international trains where a supplement is compulsory, a supplement is payable unless your coupon is endorsed "No Supplement Necessary". You may sit in any empty seat without paying for a reservation, or pay the reservation charge to secure one.

Buying on board costs extra on MÁV — a surcharge applies both to buying a FIP discounted ticket and to upgrading from 2nd to 1st class. On GySEV, FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought on board at all.

Breaking your journey is permitted without formality on MÁV. On GySEV the ticket must be endorsed at the station where you break.

Changing your route is not possible on the train — only at a ticket office before you start, and the original ticket becomes invalid with a surcharge on the new one.

How far ahead: international tickets and reservations no more than 6 months before the first day of validity; domestic FIP discounted tickets, 60 days. Changes to international tickets bought in Hungary can only be made the same day, at the same station.

Also covered: the Szeged–Hódmezővásárhely TramTrain and the Balatonfenyves narrow-gauge line. Not covered: MÁV or GySEV buses, unless they are running as rail replacement.

🇭🇷 Croatia ▾

Operator: HŽ (Croatian Railways). One coupon a year, employee only, 50% on the FIP Card.

Supplements are payable on EC and "high comfort" services. Reservations are mandatory on IC and ICN, with a fee.

🇵🇱 Poland ▾
FIP Card 50% Coupons: staff only Supplements on fast trains

Poland is not one FIP operator — it is six. PKP (national), plus the regional railways KD (Lower Silesia), KŚ (Silesia), KW (Greater Poland) and ŁKA (Łódź) — each its own undertaking with its own coupon. KMŁ (Kraków area) appears in the FIP arrangements too, but carries no coupon allocation of its own — treat it as FIP Card territory.

What a PKP coupon actually covers: PKP services, and also Koleje Mazowieckie (KM), PKP SKM Tricity (Gdańsk/Gdynia), Warszawska Kolej Dojazdowa (WKD), and some KD routes. Outside those you need that operator's own coupon.

⚠ EIP (Pendolino) — no reservation, no travel On Express InterCity Premium trains you must have a seat reservation and pay a supplement, whether you hold a coupon or a FIP Card ticket. Travelling without a reservation is not permitted — you will pay an extra charge on top of the fare. Already reserved? You can pay the supplement on the train. Not reserved? It has to be a PKP ticket office, at the same time as reserving.
⚠ EIC, IC and TLK Where reservation is compulsory, the same rule applies. Buying on board is possible but costs a 20 PLN supplement — on IC and TLK, find the on-train staff immediately after departure.

Sleepers are a good deal: no couchette or berth charge and the reservation is free, but a service charge applies — 140 PLN single berth, 74 PLN double, 65 PLN triple. A seat in a Manager Compartment is 99 PLN.

Your ticket does not last all day on short hops: up to 50 km it is valid 3 hours; 51–100 km 6 hours; 101 km and over, until midnight.

FIP is valid on PKP rail-replacement buses, and not on PKP cable or rack railways. On Koleje Mazowieckie, FIP Card tickets are not valid on the Słoneczny summer train.

🇸🇰 Slovakia ▾

⚠ The website will show you a discount you cannot have FIP discounted tickets cannot be bought from the ZSSK website, even where the discount appears to be available there. Buy at a ticket office. On board, only if you started at a station whose ticket office is unstaffed.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming Eurostar accepts FIP coupons — it doesn't. Fixed FIP fares apply instead
  • Assuming FIP is always cheaper than public fares — compare advance tickets first
  • Not booking FIP seats early — they're limited and sell out weeks ahead on popular routes
  • Writing on a coupon too early — fill in the date as close to travel as possible, in permanent ink, no corrections
  • Using coupons on private or low-cost operators (Italo, OUIGO, Ouigo Spain, RegioJet) — these don't accept FIP
  • Assuming city metros and trams are included — they almost never are
  • Forgetting that reservations are still required on many high-speed trains even with a coupon
  • Trying to buy on board in Belgium from July 2026 — you must buy before boarding

What to carry

  • Passport
  • FIP Card (if using discounted fares)
  • FIP coupons filled in correctly (if using free travel)
  • Seat reservation or supplement confirmation where required
  • Printed or digital ticket confirmation
  • Any booking reference from Trainseurope / bookmyrst.co.uk

Important to know

  • Leisure travel only — not for commuting or any work purpose
  • Always carry your FIP Card and passport together
  • Book early — FIP seats are limited and sell out fast on popular trains
  • Eligible for you, your spouse/partner, and dependent children
  • Participating operators only — city metros, trams, buses and private rail operators are generally not in FIP. You'll need a regular ticket to get around each city
  • Coupons match your UK staff travel pass class — if you have a standard (second-class) pass, your coupons are for second class
  • FIP is not a contractual entitlement — it can change or be withdrawn at any time
  • Normal delay and cancellation terms apply — travel is at your own risk
