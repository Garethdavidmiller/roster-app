The quick answer

Most Rangers and Rovers are not valid on Chiltern. They are area passes sold by other operators for other parts of the country, and ours is a narrow corridor out of Marylebone.

Below are the Rangers and Rovers most relevant to Chiltern and Marylebone that we have identified and checked. This is not an exhaustive national list. If a product is not shown here, do not assume it is invalid — check the retail system or the current National Rail conditions.

Covers Oxford, Kingham, Moreton-in-Marsh, Charlbury — stations on a line to Oxford. It is a Great Western Railway product; Chiltern is not an operator on it, so the answer at our gateline is no.

Anything else — check, don't refuse

This page covers the products most relevant to Marylebone. It is not the national list, and National Rail's catalogue changes — so a ticket missing from here is out of scope, not invalid. Check it in the retail system. Refusing a valid pass is the worse mistake, and it is the one this page could cause.

How to check one

The date box — the check you can actually do ✓ National Rail checked · Aug 2026

Where a Rover requires self-validation, the holder completes the next travel-date box themselves, before beginning that day's journey. Numbers only, and in ink — 07 09 for the 7th of September. Follow the instructions printed on that particular Rover; the Heart of England Rover is the one this is quoted from.

An undated box on a journey in progress is the fault — the ticket has not been validated for today. A box dated in pencil, or dated after you asked, is the same thing.

This is the one rule on this page you can enforce by looking. Everything else needs the retail system.

1
Is it carded above? If yes, that card has the rules — and its status says how well sourced they are. If not, go straight to step 5: this page is not the national list.
2
Is today inside it? Day rangers are one date. Flexi rovers need today's box dated.
3
Is it too early? Most carry a 09:00 or 09:30 Mon–Fri bar. Weekends and Bank Holidays are usually clear.
4
Are we inside the area? The boundary is a station, not a region — Leamington Spa and Banbury are the two that matter to us.
5
Still unsure? Check it in the retail system before refusing. Refusing a valid pass is the worse mistake.

Two things that catch people out ✓ National Rail checked · Aug 2026

A Two Together Railcard discount adds its own time bar. Two Together discounted travel is not valid before 09:30 Monday–Friday, public holidays excepted — so a Ranger or Rover bought with that discount cannot be used before 09:30 even where the product itself would allow 09:00.

No prices on this page, deliberately. Ranger and Rover fares are re-set every year and vary three ways (adult, child, railcard). A stale price here would be a mis-sell. The retail system is the price.
