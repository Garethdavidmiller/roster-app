🗺️ Rangers & Rovers

At-work reference · area passes, and whether they work on us
⚠ Draft — not yet confirmed against National Rail

Everything below was put together from public summaries, not from the official product pages or the retail system. It is a starting point, not an authority. Before you refuse anyone travel, check the ticket in the retail system.

The quick answer

Most Rangers and Rovers are not valid on Chiltern. They are area passes sold by other operators for other parts of the country, and ours is a narrow corridor out of Marylebone.

Five are worth knowing. If the ticket in your hand is not one of these five, the answer is almost certainly no — check it in the retail system before refusing.

  • Shakespeare Explorer — ours, from Marylebone
  • Heart of England Rover — Chiltern is a named operator
  • West Midlands Day Ranger — our Birmingham end
  • Oxfordshire Day Ranger — our Bicester/Oxford branch
  • All Line Rover — valid on everything, everywhere

Near misses — NOT valid on us

⊘ Cotswolds Discoverer

Covers Oxford, Kingham, Moreton-in-Marsh, Charlbury — stations on a line to Oxford, but the ticket is CrossCountry, GWR and Transport for Wales only. Not us, even where the station is shared.

⊘ Freedom of Severn & Solent Rover

Reaches Worcester and Great Malvern in the north. TfW, GWR, South Western and CrossCountry — not Chiltern.

⊘ Anything else

If it is not one of the five above, assume it does not cover Chiltern and check the retail system. This page deliberately lists only what turns up at Marylebone — a ticket missing from this page is out of scope, not automatically invalid.

How to check one

The date box — the check you can actually do to confirm

On a flexi Rover (3 days in 7, 8 in 15 and so on) the passenger writes the date in the next empty box themselves, before they travel. Numbers only, and in ink — 07 09 for the 7th of September.

An undated box on a journey in progress is the fault — the ticket has not been validated for today. A box dated in pencil, or dated after you asked, is the same thing.

This is the one rule on this page you can enforce by looking. Everything else needs the retail system.

1
Is it one of the five? If not, it almost certainly does not cover us.
2
Is today inside it? Day rangers are one date. Flexi rovers need today's box dated.
3
Is it too early? Most carry a 09:00 or 09:30 Mon–Fri bar. Weekends and Bank Holidays are usually clear.
4
Are we inside the area? The boundary is a station, not a region — Leamington Spa and Banbury are the two that matter to us.
5
Still unsure? Check it in the retail system before refusing. Refusing a valid pass is the worse mistake.

Two things that catch people out to confirm

A Two Together Railcard discount adds its own time bar. Where a Ranger or Rover has been bought with a Two Together discount, it cannot be used before 09:30 Mon–Fri — even if the product itself would allow 09:00.

No prices on this page, deliberately. Ranger and Rover fares are re-set every year and vary three ways (adult, child, railcard). A stale price here would be a mis-sell. The retail system is the price.
