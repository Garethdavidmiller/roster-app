The quick answer

A Ranger or Rover can be valid on Chiltern without being valid at Marylebone. Most of them are area passes for other parts of the country, and several that do name Chiltern only reach the Birmingham or Oxford end of our route — they stop at Leamington Spa or Banbury and are no use for a journey to or from London.

The cards below are grouped by what matters here: whether the passenger can actually use it at Marylebone, where its Chiltern validity starts and stops, and any time or route restriction. This is a Marylebone reference, not the national Ranger and Rover catalogue. If a product is not shown, do not assume it is invalid — check the retail system.

Valid on Chiltern — but not at Marylebone

These name Chiltern as an operator, and they are real tickets on our network — just not on the London end of it. Each one's area stops well short of Marylebone, so a passenger travelling to or from London needs a separate fare for the part outside the boundary.

Anything else — check, don't refuse

This page covers the products most relevant to Marylebone, and is grouped by whether they work here. It is not the national list, and National Rail's catalogue changes — so a ticket missing from here is out of scope, not invalid. Check it in the retail system. Refusing a valid pass is the worse mistake, and it is the one this page could cause.

How to check one

The date box — the check you can actually do ✓ National Rail checked · Aug 2026

Where a Rover requires self-validation, the holder completes the next travel-date box themselves, before beginning that day's journey. Numbers only, and in ink — 07 09 for the 7th of September. Follow the instructions printed on that particular Rover; the Heart of England Rover is the one this is quoted from.

What goes in the box is not the same on every Rover — the Freedom of Severn & Solent page asks for an ✗ in the box rather than a written date. So the rule to enforce is that the box has been completed before travelling, not the particular mark. A blank one is what you are looking for.

An undated box on a journey in progress is the fault — the ticket has not been validated for today. A box dated in pencil, or dated after you asked, is the same thing.

This is the one rule on this page you can enforce by looking. Everything else needs the retail system.

Selling a Rover ✓ National Rail checked · Aug 2026

A Rover may not be issued more than 3 days ahead of the date it becomes valid — 5 days for telesales and internet bookings. This is a Rover rule, not one product's: it is on the ticket pages for the Heart of England Rover and the Freedom of Severn & Solent Rover alike. Day Rangers and Discoverers set their own — the Cotswolds Discoverer, for one, says 7 days.

1
Is it valid at Marylebone? Look at the MYB badge first. If it says ⊘, the fact that Chiltern is named on the ticket does not make it valid through this gateline.
2
Which part of Chiltern does it cover? Check the boundary station, not the operator list. "Chiltern is a participating operator" is where the check starts, never where it ends.
3
Is today inside it? Day Rangers are one date. Flexi Rovers need today's box dated — see the date-box rule above.
4
Is it too early? Most carry a 09:00 or 09:30 Mon–Fri bar, and a Railcard discount can add its own on top. Weekends and Bank Holidays are usually clear.
5
Any unusual restriction? Break of journey, named excluded services, group composition, route exclusions.
6
Still unsure? Check it in the retail system before refusing — including anything not carded here. Refusing a valid pass is the worse mistake.

Two things that catch people out ✓ National Rail checked · Aug 2026

A Two Together Railcard discount adds its own time bar. Two Together discounted travel is not valid before 09:30 Monday–Friday, public holidays excepted — so a Ranger or Rover bought with that discount cannot be used before 09:30 even where the product itself would allow 09:00.

No prices on this page, deliberately. Ranger and Rover fares are re-set every year and vary three ways (adult, child, railcard). A stale price here would be a mis-sell. The retail system is the price.
