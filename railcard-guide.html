UK Railcard Guide

Eligibility · discounts · time rules
The weekday morning rule Most railcards cannot reduce a fare below £12 on Monday–Friday before 10:00. The Network Railcard is stricter — it's completely invalid before 10:00 Mon–Fri. A handful of cards have no restriction at all — see the green cards below. Bank holidays count like weekends: restrictions don't apply.

Any two named adults — both names are printed on the card
1/3 off each Min £12 before 10:00

Common misuse: one person travelling alone. If only one passenger is present, the card is not valid — full fare applies.

Network Railcard — SE England routes only
Network Railcard
£30/yr
Anyone aged 16+ — for travel within the Network Railcard area (SE England)
Adults 1/3 off (up to 4) Children 60% off (up to 4) NOT valid before 10:00 Mon–Fri
Up to 4 adults + up to 4 children (5–15) — all must travel together with the cardholder
Unlike other railcards this is completely invalid before 10am on weekdays — not just a minimum fare. Also only covers journeys within the Network Railcard area; longer Chiltern routes beyond the SE area may fall outside its coverage.
What to check on any railcard
1
Is it in date? Check the expiry date on the card or in the app. An expired railcard gives no discount, even if it's only just expired.
2
Does the photo match the person? Disabled Persons railcards and digital railcards include a photo. Physical Senior and some older railcards do not — if age seems off, ask politely for ID.
3
Does the card type match the passenger? 16–25: should be under 26 (or a student). 26–30: should look 26–30. Senior: should look 60+. Use your judgement and ask if in doubt.
4
Are all required travelling companions present? Two Together: both named people must be there. Family & Friends: at least one child aged 5–15. Disabled Persons: cardholder and companion both present. HM Forces: family must travel with the cardholder.
5
Is it in the right format? 16–17 Saver and 26–30 are digital only — no physical versions exist. A paper "26–30 railcard" is not genuine.
Digital railcards

Digital railcards can be stored in the Railcard app (by National Rail) or in the Trainline app. Both are valid.

The official Railcard app displays an animated shimmer effect — this is hard to fake. A static screenshot saved to the camera roll is not valid, and you can ask to see the live app.

If a passenger's phone is dead and they only have a digital card, they would need to buy a full-price ticket for the journey. They can then contact National Rail to reclaim the railcard discount once they can prove their card was valid.
