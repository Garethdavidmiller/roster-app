Common gotchas
Two Together If only one of the two named passengers is travelling, the card is invalid — full fare due. Most common misuse.
Family & Friends Adults cannot use it alone — at least one child aged 5–15 must be present. Under-5s travel free anyway and do not count.
26–30 No physical card exists. A paper "26–30 Railcard" is not genuine.
16–17 Saver Expires on their 18th birthday or after 1 year, whichever comes first. Digital only.
Network Completely invalid before 10:00 Mon–Fri — not just a minimum fare. After 10:00 on weekdays, minimum adult fare is £13 (not £12). On Chiltern services, the area ends at Banbury.
Senior Age is checked when they buy it, not when they travel — so a young-looking cardholder may still be valid. Physical cards have no photo.
Digital A screenshot is not valid. Ask to see the live Railcard app — the genuine one has an animated shimmer.
No card If they don't have the railcard with them (forgotten, dead phone, lost), they must pay the full fare. They can claim a refund online within 28 days if they can prove the railcard was valid.
Under-5s Children under 5 travel free with any fare-paying adult — no railcard required, no ticket required.
How to check a railcard
1
Expiry date — printed on the card or shown in the app.
2
Photo matches the passenger. Most cards have a photo (printed on plastic or shown in the app). Senior physical cards are the exception — they carry no photo.
3
Age looks right for the card type — use judgement and ask politely for ID if in doubt.
4
Required companions present — see "Common gotchas" above.
5
Correct format — 16–17 Saver and 26–30 are digital only; no physical card exists.
Digital railcards

Digital railcards live in the official Railcard app (by National Rail) or the Trainline app. Both are valid.

The genuine Railcard app displays an animated shimmer that's very hard to fake. A static screenshot is not valid — ask to see the live app.

If their phone is dead, the passenger needs a full-fare ticket. They can claim a refund afterwards via National Rail if they can prove their card was valid.
