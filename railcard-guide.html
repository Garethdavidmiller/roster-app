At-work reference · all 12 cards
Each card's coloured stripe is its Monday–Friday morning rule — the exact rule is in the When row on the card.
Green — no time limit. Amber — a morning restriction (usually a £12 min fare before 10:00, or no travel before 09:30). Red — Network Railcard, the most limited.
All amber & red rules lift at weekends and on bank holidays.
No time limit Morning rule Mon–Fri (see card) Network: not valid before 10:00
At weekends & bank holidays, no restrictions apply on any amber or Network card — they all behave like green cards.

Common gotchas
Two Together If only one of the two named passengers is travelling, the card is invalid — full fare due. Most common misuse. They must also carry a Photocard (issued free, shows both faces) — the plastic railcard itself has no photo on it.
HM Forces The plastic Railcard has no photo on it. A separate Photocard (free, issued alongside) must always be carried too — ask for both. Without the Photocard the discount is not valid.
Family & Friends Adults cannot use it alone — at least one child aged 5–15 must be present. Under-5s travel free anyway and do not count.
26–30 No physical card exists — a paper "26–30 Railcard" is not genuine. Unlike the 16-25 card, its £12 minimum fare is not waived in July & August.
Summer waiver The £12 morning minimum fare is dropped for all of July & August on the 16-25, HM Forces and Veterans cards — they work any time those months. 26-30 is the exception — its minimum fare stays year-round. Cards with a "no travel before 09:30/10:00" rule (Network, Senior, Two Together, Family & Friends, Gold Card) have no minimum fare, so the summer waiver doesn't apply to them.
Jobcentre+ Issued by a Work Coach — not sold in ticket offices or online, and a physical card only (no app version). Valid 3 months; check the expiry. Accepted only by participating operators — Chiltern is one. Discounts day tickets and seasons up to 3 months.
16–17 Saver Expires on their 18th birthday or after 1 year, whichever comes first. Digital only.
Network Cannot be used at all before 10:00 Mon–Fri — not just a minimum fare. After 10:00 on weekdays, minimum adult fare is £13 (not £12). On Chiltern, the area ends just past Banbury at Kings Sutton — not valid to Leamington, Warwick or Birmingham.
Gold Card Not the same as the Network Railcard. It comes free with an annual season ticket, works the whole Chiltern route incl. Birmingham, and is valid from 09:30 — not 10:00. The season ticket itself has no photo — the holder must also carry a separate Photocard (free, issued at the ticket office).
Senior Age is checked when they buy it, not when they travel — so a young-looking cardholder may still be valid. Physical cards have no photo.
Season tickets Standard railcards (16-25, 26-30, Senior, Network, Two Together, Family & Friends, Disabled Persons, Veterans, HM Forces) do not discount season tickets — a common misconception. Only exceptions: Jobcentre Plus (seasons up to 3 months) and 16–17 Saver (50% off, standard class). A Gold Card comes free with an annual season ticket — it's the season ticket that earns the leisure benefits, not the railcard discounting the season.
Digital A screenshot is not valid. Ask to see the live Railcard app — the genuine one has an animated shimmer.
No card Outbound: they need a full-fare ticket before boarding. Inbound: the journey has already happened — follow your excess or penalty fare procedure. Either way, they can claim a refund online within 28 days if they can prove the railcard was valid.
Under-5s Children under 5 travel free with any fare-paying adult — no railcard required, no ticket required.
Checking railcards — gateline & selling

Same checks whether you're selling a ticket (outbound) or inspecting one at the gate (inbound). Outbound: resolve it before the passenger travels. Inbound: the journey has already happened — follow your excess or penalty fare procedure.

1
Check the time — amber and red cards, Mon–Fri only. Amber cards have a morning rule: either a £12 minimum fare before 10:00 (16-25, 26-30, HM Forces, Veterans), or no travel before 09:30 (Two Together & Gold Card; Senior & Family & Friends on Network-area journeys). Network card (red): not valid at all before 10:00. No restrictions at weekends or bank holidays.
2
Expiry date — printed on the card or shown in the app.
3
Photo check. Digital cards (Railcard app) always show a photo — check it matches the person. For physical cards: 16–25 (bought online) and Veterans have the photo printed on the card itself. HM Forces, Two Together, and annual season ticket holders (Gold Card) carry no photo on the card — they must also show a separate Photocard; ask for it if not offered. Senior, Network, Disabled Persons, and Family & Friends physical cards carry no photo at all and there is no separate Photocard — you can only check age/eligibility, not identity.
4
Age looks right for the card type — use judgement and ask politely for ID if in doubt.
5
Companions are present — Two Together: both named adults must be travelling together. Family & Friends: at least one child aged 5–15 must be in the group. Check this inbound too — adults arriving alone on group tickets are a revenue risk.
6
Digital-only cards — 16–17 Saver and 26–30 exist only as digital cards. No physical version is genuine.
Digital railcards

Digital railcards live in the official Railcard app (by National Rail) or the Trainline app. Both are valid.

The genuine Railcard app displays an animated shimmer that's very hard to fake. A static screenshot is not valid — ask to see the live app.

When selling a discounted ticket, ask to see the railcard on-screen before you price the journey.

If the phone is dead or the app won't open, treat it the same as a forgotten card — full fare outbound, or excess/penalty fare procedure inbound.
