Weekends & bank holidays — the morning time & minimum-fare limits shown here usually don't apply. But who can travel, the card's area, and any companion/child rules still apply. The When row gives the weekday cutoff.
Reading the morning time rules Some cards have a fixed national morning cutoff — Network Railcard (10:00 Mon–Fri) and Two Together (09:30 Mon–Fri) are the same everywhere. For others the rule is only "no discount in the morning peak" and the exact off-peak start varies by route — those are the ones where a shown Marylebone time is a local shortcut, not a national rule. The genuinely-varying cards below (Family & Friends, Senior, GroupSave) spell it out in three layers: Official the national rule · Marylebone the local cutoff you'll normally use · Check what the retail system confirms. When unsure, the ticket system decides.

Common gotchas

Two Together If only one named passenger is travelling, the card is invalid — full fare due. Most common misuse. Check both named adults are travelling; a physical card has a separate Two Together Photocard (both photos) that must be shown too.
HM Forces The plastic Railcard has no photo on it. A separate Photocard (free, issued alongside) must always be carried too. Without the Photocard the discount is not valid.
Family & Friends Adults cannot use it alone — at least one child aged 5–15 must be present. Under-5s exception: if the only children travelling are under 5, buy a child ticket for one of them so the adults still get the discount.
26–30 No physical card — a paper "26–30 Railcard" is not genuine. Unlike 16–25, its £12 minimum fare is not waived in July & August.
Jobcentre+ Issued by a Work Coach — not sold at ticket offices or online. Physical card only (no app). Valid 3 months — check expiry. Accepted by participating operators only; Chiltern is one.
16–17 Saver Expires on their 18th birthday or after 1 year, whichever comes first. Digital only — no physical card. Exception: a season ticket bought before the railcard expired stays valid for up to 4 months after the railcard expires.
Network Treat as no discount before 10:00 Mon–Fri — this is a complete restriction, not just a minimum fare. After 10:00, minimum adult fare is £13. Area ends after Banbury / Kings Sutton — not valid to Leamington, Warwick or Birmingham.
Gold Card Not the same as the Network Railcard. Issued with a qualifying annual season ticket, covers the whole Chiltern route incl. Birmingham, discount not valid 04:30–09:29 Mon–Fri. Evidence: a paper season shows the Gold Card + Photocard; a Smartcard/Oyster season shows a Gold Record Card (card or digital).
Senior Age is checked at purchase, not at travel — a young-looking cardholder may still be valid. Physical cards have no photo.
Season tickets Standard railcards do not discount season tickets. Only exceptions: Jobcentre Plus (seasons up to 3 months) and 16–17 Saver (50% off, standard class). A Gold Card is issued with a qualifying annual season ticket — it doesn't discount one.
Minimum fare Where a minimum fare applies — £12 on the age & Forces cards (16–25, 26–30, HM Forces, Veterans), £13 on Network — charge the higher of the discounted fare or the minimum. Senior, Two Together and Family & Friends have no adult minimum. It bites on weekday mornings and never on Advance tickets.
First Class Most cards give the same 1/3 off in First Class too. Standard class only: 16–17 Saver, Family & Friends, Jobcentre Plus, Network, and GroupSave.
Digital A screenshot is not valid — see the Digital railcards section below.
No card Outbound: full-fare ticket required before boarding. Inbound: journey has happened — follow excess or penalty fare procedure. They can claim a refund online within 28 days if they prove the railcard was valid.
Under-5s Children under 5 travel free with any fare-paying adult — no railcard, no ticket needed. But on a Family & Friends Railcard where every child is under 5, buy a discounted child ticket for one of them to unlock the adult discount.

Checking railcards — gateline & selling

Same checks whether you're selling (outbound) or inspecting at the gate (inbound). Outbound: resolve before the passenger travels. Inbound: journey has happened — follow excess or penalty fare procedure.

1
Check the time — amber and red cards, Mon–Fri only. £ = min £12 fare applies (travel allowed, must pay at least £12). ⊘ = no discount before the cutoff (full fare due). Red (Network): ⊘ before 10:00. Time restrictions don't apply at weekends or bank holidays.
2
Expiry date — printed on the card or shown in the app.
3
Photo check.
Any digital card (in the app) Photo shows in the app — check it matches
Physical 16–25 (bought online), Veterans Photo on the card — check it matches
Physical HM Forces, Gold Card Check the photo on the separate Photocard (a Smartcard/Oyster Gold season shows a Gold Record Card instead)
Physical Two Together Separate Two Together Photocard carries both photos — check it, and that both named holders are travelling
Physical Senior, Network, Disabled, F&F Usually no photo — check age/eligibility looks right
If they show a digital version of any card, check the live app — never a screenshot.
4
Age looks right for the card type — use judgement, ask politely for ID if in doubt.
5
Companions are present — Two Together: both named adults must travel together. Family & Friends: at least one child aged 5–15 must be in the group. Check this inbound too.
6
Digital-only cards — 16–17 Saver and 26–30 exist only as digital cards. No physical version is genuine.

Digital railcards

Digital railcards live in the official Railcard app (by National Rail) — this is the primary app. Some passengers carry them in the Trainline app instead; this is accepted but Trainline's display varies. Both are valid — accept either.

The official Railcard app displays an animated shimmer on the railcard — very hard to fake. A static screenshot is not valid. If they're showing a Trainline display, check the expiry date and photo carefully.

When selling a discounted ticket, ask to see the railcard on-screen before pricing the journey.

If the phone is dead or the app won't open — full fare outbound, excess/penalty fare procedure inbound.
