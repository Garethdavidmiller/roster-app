1. What it does
 
• Your roster — shifts, rest days and annual leave, month by month. Swipe between months, tap a day for the detail.
• Book annual leave, or record an absence.
• Team Week View — who is on with you.
• The Daily Huddle, Weekly Retail Circular and Marylebone Newsletter, plus guides for railcards, ranger and rover tickets, and FIP travel. All under the ☰ menu, top left.
2. Put it on your phone
 

You can just use the link in a browser. But adding it to your home screen gives you an app icon, opens it full screen, and means your roster still shows when you have no signal.

Android — open the link above in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu (top right), then Add to Home screen (it may say Install app). Confirm, and it appears alongside your other apps.

iPhone — open the link in Safari (it has to be Safari, not Chrome), tap the Share button at the bottom, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

It will not install on a work mobile at the moment. You can still open the link in the browser on a work phone and use it normally — it just will not add itself to the home screen. Use your own phone if you want the app icon.
