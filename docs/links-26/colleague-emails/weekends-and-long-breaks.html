The closest fit for you
Middle Ground
• Regular football weekends. About 14 Saturdays off a year, never more than 5 weeks apart. With your Sunday given up, each one is a full weekend.
• The cheapest three-day trips. A football weekend and a three-day trip each month takes about 6 days of leave: the fewest of the three.
• Four-day trips too. About 12 days of leave for 12 a year, the same as Level Crossing and 2 fewer than Balanced V6.

How a day of leave helps
 
• Give your Sundays up early. A Sunday is overtime, so mark yourself unavailable in advance and a colleague will take it. Then every Saturday off is a full weekend. You lose that Sunday’s overtime pay.
• A worked Saturday becomes a weekend. One day of leave on the Saturday, with the Sunday given up, and the weekend is yours.
• Two days off become three. Book a day of leave on the working day just before or just after a two-day break. Middle Ground has about 36 two-day breaks a year to choose from.
• Four days cost more. A two-day break needs two days of leave to become four, on every proposal.
• Book early. Fridays, Mondays and Saturdays are popular, and leave depends on the depot’s quota for each day.

What Middle Ground gives up
 
• Fewer free long breaks than today. About 6 three-day breaks a year with no leave, against 13 today. Most of your trips would need a day of leave.
• More leave than today. Today’s link needs the same or less leave for every year above: the same for a monthly weekend and trip, and about 3 days fewer with four-day trips.
The other two
 

Level Crossing gives the same weekends and the same four-day trips as Middle Ground. But it has fewer free breaks, so three-day trips take about 2 more days of leave a year.

Balanced V6 has the most free three-day breaks of the three, 8 a year. But more of its rest days are single days, which a day of leave cannot turn into three, so every year above takes 2 to 4 more days of leave than on Middle Ground.

Your leave allowance
 

On the main CEA contract you have 32 days of leave a year. On Middle Ground, a football weekend and a three-day trip each month would use about 6 of them, leaving about 26 for holidays. With four-day trips it would use about 12, leaving about 20.

They assume the leave is available when you want it, that you give up the Sundays you need, and leave out cover weeks, the spare weeks whose four shifts are set nearer the time.
