# 30 days off for 14 days' leave

The links in this folder are the 26-line proposals where **14 days' annual leave buys 30 days off in a row, and four
complete weeks off take 14 days' leave — as on today's link**. Every other proposal in the pack buys 27 to 29 days, and
needs 14 or 15 days' leave for four weeks off.

They were put in a folder of their own on 4 November 2026 (owner: "Put all of the links that give 30 days off for 14
days AL into a subfolder within links 26"). They are still part of the pack: the summary sheet, the shortlist sheet's
comparisons and the league count them like every other proposal. Common Ground has been on the shortlist since
5 November 2026, as the example of the links that keep today's leave (owner); the other four are not.

Each has the same three files as the proposals in `../proposals/`: the eight-page sheet (`<Name>-<CODE>-<fingerprint>.pdf`),
the grid (`<Name>-<CODE>.json`) and the text for the Links page's Import (`<Name>-<CODE>-import.txt`).

**What the 30 days cost.** Every layout found that buys 30 days for 14 has more single rest days than Even Step and
Short Run, the other two shortlisted links: 10 or 12 a year each here, against their 8 (today about 10). Open Return and
Short Openers also have a 13h 15m shortest rest, where the shortlisted three have 14h 20m or more; Grand Tour has 16
weeks of 21 on one shift time, against Fair Share's 18, and a 56h 40m week if a cover week falls badly. Common Ground
has Grand Tour's weeks, so its 12 single rest days a year too, and a weaker weekday fit to the trains than the other two
shortlisted links. Middle Ground has Common Ground's rest days and duties, so the same 12 and the same fit, and pays for
spreading its closing and 06:20 weeks apart with one week fewer on one shift time (18 of 21) and a larger change of
start from week to week.

**Common Ground** joined the folder on the evening of 4 November 2026. It was drawn up in another session, on the
owner's brief for today's link taken to 26 weeks, and reached the pack through `main` (#1690) while this folder was being
changed; it buys 30 days for 14, so it belongs here too. Its weeks are Grand Tour's, all 182 cells working or resting
alike. Seven of its thirteen shift times are worked today; the other six are new — shorter versions of three of
today's lates, a 07:00–15:30 early and two Sunday earlies. (Until 6 November 2026 it had fourteen, the fourteenth a
9-hour Saturday late, 13:30–22:30, which now finishes at 22:00: below.)

**Middle Ground** joined the folder on 5 November 2026 (owner: "Can you rename good turn as Middle Ground and add it to
the pack please"). It was drawn for the owner's point that Common Ground's closing weeks and its 06:20 weeks come in
runs, which makes swapping a week with a colleague harder: it keeps Common Ground's rest days and its early and late
weeks, and chooses again which week works which shift time, so that no two closing weeks and no two 06:20 weeks fall
side by side. It is the only design in the pack where neither does. It is not on the shortlist.

**The Saturday ticket office to 22:30** (6 November 2026; owner: "I have confirmed that it does close at 22.10 and needs
to be a 22.30 ticket office closer. Do A on every proposal"). The office shuts at 22:10 on a Saturday and takes 20
minutes to cash up, so on every link here its two Saturday lates finish at 22:30, not 22:00, and the Saturday floor
pair that finished at 22:30 finishes at 22:00 instead: Common Ground's and Middle Ground's 13:30–22:30, and Grand
Tour's, Short Openers' and Open Return's 14:00–22:30. The minutes balance, so the contract, the leave, the rest days
and the weeks are unchanged; the fingerprints, the shift times, the heaviest weeks and the league moved, and the table
gives them as they are now. Common Ground and Middle Ground now have nothing over 8h 30m on any day, as Grand Tour has,
and Common Ground is first in the main league of all nineteen. Today's roster still has its office lates leaving at
22:00. The same change was made on every proposal in the pack: `../README.md` → "The Saturday ticket office to 22:30".
